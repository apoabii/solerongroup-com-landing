Risks and costs

Based on company information and source reports provided on 20 September 2026.

Trading risk

The strategies operate in crypto markets and may use leveraged derivatives. Price movements, leverage, liquidity, order execution and exchange operations can lead to substantial losses. Risk controls do not provide a fixed loss limit or guaranteed returns.

Trading costs

Exchange trading and funding fees may vary by account, product and trading conditions. Investor-specific contractual costs are agreed in writing by the relevant parties before trading begins.

Scope of historical reports

The charts cover different bot reporting periods ending in August 2026. The company reports state that the results were compiled from live exchange records. Their presentation on this site does not imply independent verification of the raw records. Different capital bases and reporting periods mean that absolute profit amounts are not a like-for-like return comparison.

According to the report, net profit is after trading and funding fees. Investor-specific contractual costs are excluded. The maximum drawdown methodology is not specified in detail. Quantara’s components total ₮487,098 USDT, while reported net profit is ₮487,099 USDT. This site retains the reported amount and explains the ₮1 USDT discrepancy.

Scope of simulations

The ₮100,000 USDT examples in the source documents are hypothetical NAV-based allocations, not individual investor account statements. Their assumptions should not be treated as equivalent to current trading terms. This site does not present those hypothetical allocations as live account results.

Corporate structure and trading terms

The ADGM holding company and related companies are being established. This does not confirm completed registration, licensing or operating authorisation. SOLERON does not directly receive or custody users’ trading capital. Exchange account structure, withdrawal terms, minimum capital and activation requirements remain to be clarified.
