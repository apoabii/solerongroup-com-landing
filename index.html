Technology

Infrastructure connecting market data to trading decisions

SOLERON develops automated trading bots that operate on crypto exchanges. The documented architecture combines order-book data with AI-supported analysis and order management.

The model architecture describes LSTM, Transformer and reinforcement learning components. This does not imply that every component operates identically across all bots.

DATA

Liquidity alongside price

Order-book imbalance and the rate of change in market depth provide additional decision inputs.

MODEL

Assessment by market regime

The documented LSTM, Transformer and reinforcement learning architecture is intended to assess different market regimes.

EXECUTION

From signal to order

Adaptive order routing and a risk budget for each trade support execution on the exchange.

These mechanisms are described in the sources. No separate analysis has been provided to measure each mechanism’s contribution to reported profit.
